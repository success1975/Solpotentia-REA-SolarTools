📐 Methodology & Engineering Assumptions (click to expand)

This tool is designed to support preliminary off-grid rural electrification planning. It applies standard solar PV system design principles commonly used in off-grid and mini-grid project development. Peak Sun Hour data is sourced from NASA POWER (power.larc.nasa.gov) and NIMET (Nigerian Meteorological Agency) long-term irradiance records. All outputs should be treated as planning estimates and validated by a certified solar engineer before procurement.

Solar Array (Wp)
= (Daily Load Wh ÷ System Eff.) × Safety ÷ PSH
Eff=80%, Safety=1.15, PSH=location-based
Battery Bank (Ah)
= (Daily Load × Autonomy Days) ÷ (DoD × Sys Voltage)
DoD: 50% flooded | 60% AGM | 80% LiFePO4
Inverter (kW)
= Peak Simultaneous Load × 1.25 (surge factor)
25% headroom for startup surge currents
Charge Controller (A)
= (Array Wp ÷ (Sys V × 1.25)) × 1.25
MPPT sizing with 25% safety margin
